# Inspecting a synthetic dataset

1. Match the dataset name, version, map, seed and resolution to the dataset card.
2. Keep source-run statistics separate from the smaller packaged sample.
3. Compare aligned RGB, identity, depth and overlay previews at boundaries and occlusions.
4. Verify the archive SHA-256 before extracting it.
5. Sample annotations across classes, object scales and image edges.
6. Measure class balance, viewpoint coverage and perceptual similarity between splits.
7. Read every failed check and known limitation before deciding fitness for your task.
8. Record the version, hash and any exclusions beside your experiment.

The public sample is evidence for evaluation, not a benchmark or a guarantee of performance on real data.
